What the agent did

Each show has its own AI agent. It can only act inside the five money rules below. Times follow the demo’s clock, which jumps when you skip ahead.

    • Rule 1Fans’ money is only held, never charged early.
    • Rule 2When a show gets enough fans, everyone is charged at once.
    • Rule 3At the deadline: pay the room, then the opener, then the host. The artist gets the rest.
    • Rule 4If the deadline passes short, every hold is cancelled.
    • Rule 5Anything else, like a new deadline or target, the artist approves first.
