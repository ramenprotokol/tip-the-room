Questions

What does “on hold” mean?

PayPal sets $15 aside on your account, like a hotel deposit. It isn’t charged unless the show happens.

When am I charged?

Only once 21 fans have joined, and only if that happens by Thu Nov 12, 6pm. Then everyone is charged at once, and the room and the artist are paid at the deadline.

What if not enough fans join?

Every hold is cancelled automatically at the deadline. Nobody pays anything, and you don’t need to do a thing.
